OpenDP contributors are asked to sign our [Contributor License Agreement](https://docs.opendp.org/en/stable/contributing/cla.html) via a Githb workflow. Contributors retain copyright, but have granted to Harvard a broad license going beyond the terms of the MIT License under which the software is released.

Currently, our [clabot](https://github.com/opendp/clabot-config/) confirms that each contribution has a CLA on file, but this was not in place in the early years of the project and there are some discrepancies.

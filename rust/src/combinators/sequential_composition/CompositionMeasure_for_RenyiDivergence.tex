\subsection*{Postcondition}
\begin{theorem}
  \texttt{composability} returns \texttt{Ok(out)} if the composition of a vector of privacy parameters \texttt{d\_mids}
  is bounded above by \texttt{self.compose(d\_mids)} under \texttt{adaptivity} adaptivity and \texttt{out}-composability.
  Otherwise returns an error.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  The new curve constructed on line \ref{line:curve} composes all epsilon parameters at a given fixed \texttt{alpha}.
  By the postcondition of \rustdoc{traits/trait}{InfAdd} we have that for any choice of \texttt{alpha}, $\sum_i \texttt{d\_mid}_i(\texttt{alpha}) \le \texttt{compose(d\_mids)}(\alpha)$.

  For each fixed $\alpha>1$, Theorem~1.22(2) of \cite{haney2025concurrent} establishes validity of the additive interactive RDP accumulator.
  Consequently, a filter enforcing the composed curve pointwise against a public target curve is at least as restrictive as each fixed-order filter.
  The additional rejections are postprocessing of the public privacy parameters and the analyst's view.
  The guarantee thus holds at every order.

  \begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{ r | l l }
      Adaptivity & Sequential & Concurrent \\
      \hline
      Non-Adaptive & Proposition~1\cite{mironov2017renyi} & Theorem~2\cite{lyu2022interactive} \\
      Adaptive & Proposition~1\cite{mironov2017renyi} & Theorem~2\cite{lyu2022interactive} \\
      Fully-Adaptive & Theorem~4.3\cite{feldman2022individual} & Theorem~1.22\cite{haney2025concurrent}
    \end{tabular}

    This table is reflected in the implementation of \texttt{composability} on line~\ref{line:composability}.
  \end{center}

\end{proof}

\subsection*{Postcondition}
\begin{theorem}
    \label{postcondition}

    \begin{itemize}
        \item If \texttt{scale} is zero, returns the indices of the best scores by deterministic peeling, after finite clamping and optional negation.
        \item Otherwise, if replacement is set, returns a sample from $\mathcal{M}_{EM}$ (as defined in MS2020 Definition 4),
            otherwise returns a sample from $\mathcal{M}_{PF}$ (as defined in MS2020 Lemma 1),
            $\min(k, |x|)$ times by peeling the clamped and optionally negated scores,
            where $\texttt{scale} = \frac{2 \cdot \Delta}{\epsilon}$.
        \item Errors are data-independent, except for exhaustion of entropy.
    \end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    By the preconditions, clamping the scores to finite bounds and converting them and the scale to rationals is infallible.

    By the postcondition of \rustdoc{measurements/noisy_top_k/fn}{peel\_permute\_and\_flip},
    and the potential negation on line~\ref{negate},
    the postcondition is satisfied.

    The only source of error is due to entropy exhaustion.
\end{proof}

Proves soundness of \rustdoc{measurements/fn}{make\_randomized\_response} in \asOfCommit{mod.rs}{f5bb719},
a constructor taking a category set \texttt{categories} and probability \texttt{prob}.
The mechanism returned by \texttt{make\_randomized\_response} takes in a data set \texttt{arg} (a single category), and...

\begin{itemize}
    \item ...if \texttt{arg} is in \texttt{categories},
    the mechanism mixes truthful reporting with a uniform draw from the category set.
    The probability of returning the true category is approximately \texttt{prob}, rounded in the direction that increases privacy.
    \item ...if \texttt{arg} is not in \texttt{categories},
    it returns a category chosen uniformly at random.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Postcondition}
\begin{theorem}
\validMeasurement{\texttt{(categories, prob, T)}}{\\ \texttt{make\_randomized\_response}}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    Let $t\geq 2$ be the number of categories and $q$ the effective uniform-mixture probability, obtained by rounding the requested mixture probability upward to the Bernoulli grid.
    Its binary64 representation is exact, as established by the threshold conversion.
    At the uniform boundary $q=1$; at $q=0$ the reported infinite privacy loss is valid.
    For $q>0$, every output category $y$ has probability
    \[
        \Pr[M(x)=y]=
        \begin{cases}
            1-q+q/t & x=y,\\
            q/t & x\ne y,\ x\in\texttt{categories},\\
            1/t & x\notin\texttt{categories}.
        \end{cases}
    \]
    These probabilities lie between $q/t$ and $1-q+q/t$.
    The pointwise likelihood ratio, and hence the ratio for any event, is at most
    \[
        \frac{1-q+q/t}{q/t}=1+\frac{t(1-q)}q.
    \]
    The privacy map rounds the logarithm of this bound upward using directed arithmetic.
    Equal inputs have identical output distributions and zero privacy loss.
    All fallible sampling operations depend only on public parameters and random bits.
\end{proof}
